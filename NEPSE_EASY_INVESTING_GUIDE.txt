SECTION 1: THE EXACT DAILY TRADING CLOCK (WHAT TO DO & WHEN)
================================================================================

Trading NEPSE profitably is 80% discipline and timing. Follow this exact daily
routine to eliminate emotional mistakes:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 1: 09:30 AM – 10:30 AM (PRE-MARKET PREPARATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. Open the App on your phone or PC.
  2. Look at the Top Banner on the Dashboard:
     - IF RED "SYSTEMIC CASH DEFENSE ACTIVE" appears:
       -> Market breadth is weak (Declines >= 60% or Advances < 35%).
       -> Strict Rule: NO FRESH BUYING TODAY. Keep 100% cash safe.
     - IF GREEN / NORMAL:
       -> Proceed to examine today's opportunities.
  3. Go to "Daily Prime Pick" (Dashboard / Predictor Hub):
     - The app scans 350+ NEPSE stocks and displays the single best setup.
     - Check the 3 Golden Quality Filters:
       [A] Setup Score >= 58 (e.g., 65.3 or higher).
       [B] Net Win Rate >= 50% (Bayesian backtested).
       [C] Evidence Confidence: HIGH or MEDIUM (4+ analogs).
  4. Tap "Analyze Setup" to open the Entry/Exit Analyzer:
     - Check the Big Verdict Banner:
       * "BUY / ACCUMULATE" -> Approved for execution.
       * "NO TRADE (UNFAVORABLE RISK/REWARD)" -> BLOCKED (like HDHPC). Do NOT buy.
  5. Write down these 4 key numbers in your notebook/trading journal:
     - Suggested Entry Zone (e.g., Rs. 261.1 - 270.3)
     - Target 1 (e.g., Rs. 292.6)
     - Target 2 (e.g., Rs. 327.3)
     - Stop Loss (e.g., Rs. 234.9)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 2: 10:30 AM – 11:00 AM (PRE-OPEN SESSION ON TMS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. Log into your NEPSE Broker TMS portal (e.g., Broker 58, Broker 45, etc.).
  2. DO NOT place aggressive market buy orders in the pre-open session.
     Pre-open pricing in NEPSE is easily manipulated with low-volume orders.
  3. Set a Price Alert on your phone/broker for the "Pivot Entry Price"
     (e.g., Alert at Rs. 261.5 for GBIME).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 3: 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM (MARKET OPEN & BREAKOUT TRIGGER WINDOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. First 10 Minutes (11:00 - 11:10 AM): SIT ON YOUR HANDS.
     Let the opening volatility, overnight orders, and wide spreads settle.
  2. Between 11:15 AM and 11:30 AM: Check the live price and volume:
     CASE A: BREAKOUT ENTRY (Suggested Entry is above Market Price, e.g. 261.1 - 270.3):
       - If price crosses above Rs. 261.1 AND trading volume is surging (RVOL > 1.25x):
         -> Place your BUY LIMIT order between Rs. 261.1 and Rs. 268.0.
       - If price never crosses Rs. 261.1: DO NOT BUY. Keep your cash in your account.
       - THE CHASE CAP: If the stock instantly gaps up beyond Rs. 270.3 (+5% or circuit):
         -> CANCEL AND WALK AWAY. Never chase a stock past its Chase Cap!
     CASE B: PULLBACK / SUPPORT BUY (Suggested Entry is at or below Market Price):
       - If price dips into the lower support corridor on low volume:
         -> Accumulate in 2 tranches (50% now, 50% if it retests support).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 4: 11:30 AM – 01:30 PM (MID-DAY HOLDING & MONITORING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. Do NOT stare at the live ticker every minute. Checking too often creates fear.
  2. Check once an hour (at 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM):
     - Is the stock holding above the Stop Loss level?
     - If yes, do nothing. Let the position work.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 5: 01:30 PM – 02:45 PM (POWER HOUR & SMART MONEY TRACKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. This is when institutional NEPSE players make their biggest moves.
  2. Open the app and check the Floorsheet / Broker Activity:
     - Check LBAS (Large Block Absorption Share): Are blocks >= 45%?
     - Check Broker AD Ratio: Is it positive (+0.10+)?
     - If big brokers are accumulating, the breakout is authentic.
  3. Watch Target 1:
     - If the stock surges and reaches Target 1 (e.g. Rs. 292.6):
       -> Prepare to execute Profit Tranche 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 6: 02:45 PM – 03:00 PM (MARKET CLOSE EXECUTION WINDOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. EXIT FOR PROFIT:
     - If price touched or exceeded Target 1:
       -> Place a sell limit order for 50% of your shares. Lock in real cash profit.
       -> Move your Stop Loss on the remaining 50% shares to your original BUY PRICE.
       -> The trade is now 100% RISK-FREE.
  2. EXIT FOR LOSS (CAPITAL PRESERVATION):
     - If the stock broke down and closed below your Stop Loss (e.g. Rs. 234.9):
       -> SELL 100% IMMEDIATELY before 3:00 PM.
       -> Never hold overnight hoping for a miracle tomorrow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIME 7: 03:15 PM – 04:00 PM (POST-MARKET REVIEW & JOURNALING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What to do:
  1. Write your executed trades in your trade journal.
  2. Record: Buy Price, Sell Price, Target 1 locked profit, remaining shares,
     and updated Breakeven Stop Loss.
  3. Clear your mind and relax until tomorrow morning.

SECTION 2: COMPLETE RULES FOR ENTRY, HOLD, AND EXIT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE A: HOW TO ENTER FOR MAXIMUM PROFIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Never buy blindly. A trade is only valid when ALL 4 conditions match:
  1. Setup Score >= 58 with Evidence Confidence = HIGH or MEDIUM.
  2. Setup Verdict says "BUY / ACCUMULATE".
     (If it says "NO TRADE / CAPITAL PRESERVATION", DO NOT TOUCH IT).
  3. Risk-to-Reward Ratio (Target 1) is at least 1.5 : 1 (ideally >= 1.75 : 1).
  4. Entry is strictly executed WITHIN the Suggested Entry Zone, never above Chase Cap.

Position Sizing (The 2% Rule):
  - Never risk losing more than 2% of your total portfolio on one trade.
  - Formula:
      Shares to Buy = (Total Portfolio Value x 0.02) / (Buy Price - Stop Loss Price)
  - Example:
      Portfolio = Rs. 5,00,000.
      2% max risk = Rs. 10,000.
      Buy Price = Rs. 262.00, Stop Loss = Rs. 235.00 -> Risk per share = Rs. 27.00.
      Number of shares to buy = 10,000 / 27 = 370 shares.
      Total cost = 370 x 262 = Rs. 96,940 (~19% of portfolio).
      If trade fails and hits stop: You only lose Rs. 9,990 (exactly 2%).
      You still have 98% of your money ready for the next winner!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE B: HOW TO HOLD STOCKS SAFELY (NO PANIC, NO GUESSWORK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Once bought, your shares are locked for T+2 days in NEPSE settlement.
     Do not panic over minor 1% daily wiggles during T+2.
  2. While the stock trades between Entry Zone and Target 1:
     -> HOLD. Do not sell early out of impatience.
  3. When RSI is between 50 and 65:
     -> The stock is in healthy upward expansion. Let profits ride.
  4. If RSI reaches >= 75:
     -> Overbought exhaustion is near. Prepare to take profits at Target 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE C: HOW TO EXIT FOR MAXIMUM PROFIT (THE 2-TRANCHE STRATEGY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most retail traders turn winning trades into losing trades because they don't
know when to sell. Follow the 2-Tranche Strategy:

  TRANCHE 1 (At Target 1 - e.g. Rs. 292.6):
    - Sell exactly 50% of your shares.
    - Transfer that profit to your bank or cash balance.
    - IMMEDIATELY RAISE YOUR STOP LOSS to your purchase price (Rs. 261.1).
    - Even if the market crashes the next day, you exit the rest at breakeven.
      You cannot lose money on this trade!

  TRANCHE 2 (At Target 2 - e.g. Rs. 327.3):
    - Let the remaining 50% shares run until they hit Target 2.
    - Sell the remaining 50% shares at Target 2 for maximum multi-week gains.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE D: HOW TO EXIT FOR LOSS (CUTTING LOSSES LIKE A PRO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The Stop-Loss is non-negotiable. It is your financial seatbelt.
  2. If a daily candle closes below the Stop-Loss price:
     -> SELL 100% OF YOUR POSITION. No excuses, no hope, no delays.
  3. The Math of Why You Must Cut Losses Early:
     - A -5% loss only needs a +5.3% gain on the next trade to recover.
     - A -10% loss only needs a +11.1% gain on the next trade to recover.
     - A -32% loss (like HDHPC at Rs. 335) needs a massive +47% rally to recover!
     - A -50% loss needs a staggering +100% rally (doubling your money) just to break even!
  4. Never "average down" on a stock breaking its stop-loss.
     Averaging down turns a small 5% cut into a 4-year stuck position.
