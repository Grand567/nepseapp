NEPSE Alpha Playbook: Investor & Trader Guide

Actionable Framework for Profitable Execution & Systematic Capital Preservation
Core Market Reality of NEPSE: In the Nepal Stock Exchange, capital defense accounts for 80% of net profitability. Because NEPSE enforces a T+2 settlement freeze (stocks bought today cannot be sold for 2 business days), has no shorting mechanism, and caps price moves with ±10% circuit limits, wrong entries cannot be cut intraday. To extract consistent profits among the app's 110+ features, follow the hierarchy: Defend Capital First → Follow Institutional Footprints → Execute Asymmetrically.

1. Feature Reliability Classification (The 3 Tiers)

Tier 1: Non-Negotiable Capital Protectors
Weight: 90%+ Reliability

These features protect your portfolio from catastrophic drawdowns and traps.

1. Cash Defense Mode (Market Breadth Gate)
Evaluates real-time Advance/Decline ratio and New Highs vs Lows. During bear regimes (breadth < 40%), over 75% of chart breakouts fail immediately.
RULE: In Cash Defense, DO NOT BUY ANY BREAKOUT. Maintain 70-80% cash.
2. Chase Cap Guard (+2.5% Maximum Limit)
Guards against NEPSE's T+2 trap. Buying a stock already up +4% to +8% on Day 1 makes you easy exit liquidity for swing traders.
RULE: If Current Price > Entry Pivot + 2.5%, CANCEL TRADE. Never chase.
3. Promoter Lock-in Expiry Radar (60-Day Blackout)
Hydropower and Microfinance promoter shares unlock 3 years post-IPO, dumping massive supply and triggering multi-month 20-40% drawdowns.
RULE: Automatic blacklist. Never swing trade within 60 days of lock-in expiry.
4. Broker Floorsheet LBAS (Large-Block Absorption)
Tracks institutional broker accumulation (e.g. Brokers 58, 45, 34, 17). Confirms if smart money is accumulating or retail is FOMOing into distribution.
RULE: Only swing trade when Top 3 buyer brokers absorb ≥ 35% of total volume.
Tier 2: High-Edge Opportunity Engines
Statistical Edge: 80%+

The primary alpha-generating tools during neutral and bullish market conditions.

1. Day Prime Pick + Zero-Loss Protocol
Screens the market using 500-session analog pattern vectors, filtering for liquidity, fundamental health, and institutional accumulation.
ZERO-LOSS RULE: Sell 50% at Target 1 (+4% to +6%). Immediately move Stop Loss on remaining 50% to Entry Price. Trade is now immune to loss.
2. Sector Rotation Matrix & Heatmap
NEPSE capital flows in waves across sub-indices (Hydro → Finance → Commercial Banks).
RULE: Never buy a stock in a "Lagging" sector. Only enter stocks positioned in "Leading" or "Improving" quadrants.
3. Pre-Open Order Book Imbalance (OBIR)
Measures real matching bid/ask depth between 10:30 AM & 11:00 AM before the opening bell.
RULE: OBIR > 1.8 with positive depth confirms strong institutional morning demand.
4. Dual-Gate Alerts (RVOL ≥ 1.4x + VCP)
Detects volatility compression breakouts validated against a 20-day baseline relative volume.
RULE: Volume precedes price in NEPSE. Never buy a chart breakout if RVOL < 1.0x.
Tier 3: Informational Traps to Avoid in Isolation
Danger: High Failure Rate
• Standalone Oscillators (RSI / MACD / Stochastics): In speculative NEPSE runs, RSI > 70 can stay overbought for 3 weeks while the stock doubles; RSI < 30 in a downtrend can keep falling another 30%. Never trade indicators without volume and floorsheet confirmation.
• Theoretical Monte Carlo Price Projections: Statistical models cannot predict sudden Nepal Rastra Bank directives, margin loan ceiling adjustments, or interest rate policy shifts.
• Unadjusted Historical Charts: Always ensure technical levels are adjusted for bonus and right share splits.

2. The 4-Step Daily Execution Routine

10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
Step 1: Pre-Market Breadth & Blacklist
  • Check Cash Defense Mode on top banner. If ACTIVE, close TMS buy tab; hold 70-80% cash.
  • Review Day Prime Pick card: record Entry Zone, Chase Cap, Stop Loss, and Target 1.
  • Run Safety Audit: verify candidate stock has NO promoter lock-in expiry in next 60 days.
10:30 AM – 11:00 AM
Step 2: Pre-Open Order Book Validation
  • Observe Pre-Open OBIR (Order Book Imbalance Ratio) for morning buyer pressure.
  • Check matching pre-open price:
    • Gapping up > 2.5% above Entry Pivot? Abort buy (violates Chase Cap).
    • Opening inside Entry Zone with OBIR > 1.5? Confirmed.
11:15 AM – 01:30 PM
Step 3: Live Execution & Sizing
  • Wait out the 11:00–11:15 AM opening shakeout to avoid emotional retail whipsaws.
  • Verify live volume pacing: RVOL must be ≥ 1.2x – 1.5x of 20-day average.
  • Apply 1.5% Risk Rule:
    Max Shares = (Portfolio × 0.015) / (Entry - StopLoss)
  • Place Limit Orders strictly inside the Entry Zone on TMS.
03:15 PM – 04:00 PM
Step 4: Post-Market Floorsheet Audit
  • Open Floorsheet Analytics for the stock.
  • Identify Top 5 buyer and seller broker IDs: did Broker 58, 45, 34, 17 net accumulate?
  • Did Top 3 buyer brokers control ≥ 35% of total volume?
  • Calibrate trailing stop loss and lock in T+2 execution plan.
