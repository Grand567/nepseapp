0.2 Mathematical Price Geometry: Why Entry and Breakout Prices Dynamic Change

Investors often ask: Why does the Entry Price or Breakout Price recalculate between sessions?

The system computes Dynamic Execution Geometry, not static price lines: 1. LTP Anchoring & Volatility Calibration: Entry zones are calibrated to the Last Traded Price (LTP) and the 14-day Average True Range (ATR). As market volatility expands or contracts, the clearance buffer dynamically adjusts:

Clearance Buffer = \max\Big(SafeHigh_{20 × 0.0035,\; ATR_{14 × 0.22\Big)

  1. Rolling 20-Session Swing High Pivot (SafeHigh_{20): The breakout level is anchored to the highest high of the prior 20 trading sessions. As each trading day closes, older sessions roll out of the 20-day lookback window, shifting the pivot level.

  2. Pullback / Dip Zone Anchoring: The optimal dip-buying zone is anchored to the 20-session Exponential Moving Average (EMA-20) and recent 5-day base support, bounded securely above the structural stop loss:

    Pullback Anchor = EMA_{20   or   \max\Big(Structural Stop × 1.015,\; LTP × 0.98\Big)

  3. Breakout Chase Cap: To prevent retail traders from falling into the NEPSE T+2 delivery lockup trap, the engine enforces an invariant Chase Cap (+2.5\% above the pivot). Buying above this cap is mathematically prohibited by the analyzer.

3. Smart Money, Floorsheet & Order Flow Microstructure

3.1 Broker Concentration Ratios (BCR3 & BCR5)

  • Definition: The percentage share of total session volume bought or sold by the top 3 or top 5 brokerages:

    BCR3 = \frac{Volume of Top 3 Brokers{Total Session Traded Volume × 100\%

  • Thresholds:

  • BCR3 Buy ≥ 40.0\%: Institutional cornering confirmed.
  • BCR3 Sell ≥ 45.0\%: Institutional dumping warning.
⚠️ WARNING
**The Asymmetry Principle**: A high BCR3 Buy is **meaningless** if BCR3 Sell is higher. If Broker #58 buys 40\% of a stock, but Brokers #45, #34, and #28 collectively sell 55\%, the stock is experiencing **net institutional distribution**.

3.2 Distribution Trap (Pump & Dump Architecture)

  • Definition: A deceptive condition where a stock's price is green (+1\% to +6\%), but the floorsheet reveals that top brokers are dumping massive block quantities into retail market orders.
  • Identification Rules:
  • BCR3 Sell ≥ 45.0\%.
  • BCR3 Sell - BCR3 Buy ≥ 10.0\%.
  • Top 3 selling brokers are liquidating > 20\% of total session sell turnover.
  • Action: When flagged 🚨 DISTRIBUTION TRAP: DO NOT BUY, fresh capital must be completely withheld.

3.3 Stealth / Quiet Accumulation

  • Definition: Systematic institutional buying executed without causing rapid price spikes.
  • Markers:
  • Compressed daily price range (≤ 1.5\%).
  • Steady positive On-Balance Volume (OBV).
  • High buy ticket size compared to sell ticket size.

3.4 Ticket Ratio (Trade Size Asymmetry)

  • Definition:

    Ticket Ratio = \frac{Average Shares per Buy Contract{Average Shares per Sell Contract

  • Interpretation:

  • > 1.50x: Institutional accumulation (Whales buying large 2,000–5,000 kitta blocks from retail selling 50–100 kitta lots).
  • < 0.70x: Institutional offloading (Whales dumping large blocks into small retail bids).

3.5 Bilateral Cross-Trade Neutralization

  • Definition: Matched transactions where the same broker ID is on both the buy and sell side of a contract (e.g., Broker #58 selling 50,000 kitta to Broker #58).
  • Usage: The app neutralizes self-matched trades to prevent artificial inflation of accumulation scores. These represent promoter transfers or internal rebalancing, not open-market demand.

3.6 5-Depth Order Book & OBIR (Order Book Imbalance Ratio)

  • Definition: Real-time aggregation of top 5 buy bids and top 5 sell asks:

    OBIR = \frac{Total Bid Quantity (Levels 1–5){Total Ask Quantity (Levels 1–5)

  • Interpretation:

  • OBIR > 2.0x: Strong immediate buy-side liquidity cushion.
  • OBIR < 0.5x: Overhead sell resistance dominating the book.

6. Candlestick Anatomy & The 28 Japanese Candlestick Patterns

6.1 Anatomical Structure & The Physics of a Candlestick

Every Japanese candlestick contains 4 fundamental price points (OHLC) that convey the battle between buying demand and selling supply:

       High ─────────── ┬  Upper Shadow / Wick (Supply Overhead / Intraday Rejection)
                        │  • Upper Wick = High - max(Open, Close)
      Close ┌─────────┐ │
            │         │ ┼  Real Body (True Directional Institutional Conviction)
            │  GREEN  │ │  • Real Body = |Close - Open|
            │         │ │  • Green (Bullish): Close > Open
       Open └─────────┘ │  • Red (Bearish): Close < Open
                        │
        Low ─────────── ┴  Lower Shadow / Tail (Dip Absorption / Demand Floor)
                           • Lower Wick = min(Open, Close) - Low

The 5 Primary Candlestick Metrics Calculated in Code:

  1. Total Candle Range (Spread):

    Range = High - Low

  2. Real Body Ratio (R_{body):

    R_{body = \frac{|Close - Open|{High - Low

  3. R_{body ≥ 0.82: Marubozu (one-way institutional momentum).

  4. 0.25 ≤ R_{body ≤ 0.55: Standard balanced trading.
  5. R_{body ≤ 0.14: Doji (equilibrium, supply/demand standoff).

  6. Upper Wick Ratio (R_{upper):

    R_{upper = \frac{High - \max(Open, Close){High - Low

  7. R_{upper ≥ 0.62: Massive overhead selling rejection (Shooting Star / Gravestone Doji).

  8. Lower Tail Ratio (R_{lower):

    R_{lower = \frac{\min(Open, Close) - Low{High - Low

  9. R_{lower ≥ 0.62: Strong institutional buying absorption (Hammer / Dragonfly Doji).

  10. Gap Distance (\Delta_{gap):

    Δ_{gap = \frac{Open - Close_{prev{Close_{prev × 100\%

  11. \Delta_{gap ≥ +1.5\%: Rising Window or Bullish Kicker impulse.

  12. \Delta_{gap ≤ -1.5\%: Falling Window or Bearish Kicker shock.

9. Fundamentals, Solvency & Valuation Frameworks

9.1 EPS, P/E & PBV Multiples

  • EPS (Earnings Per Share): Net Profit After Tax / Total Outstanding Shares.
  • P/E (Price-to-Earnings): LTP / EPS. Compare against Sector P/E.
  • PBV (Price-to-Book Value): LTP / Book Value Per Share (BVPS). Measures asset backing.

9.2 Banking Solvency: NPL & CAR (NRB Regulatory Directives)

  • NPL (Non-Performing Loan Ratio): Percentage of loans overdue > 90 days.
  • NPL ≤ 3.5\%: Healthy bank.
  • NPL > 5.0\%: Severe Red Flag. Nepal Rastra Bank prohibits banks with NPL > 5.0\% from distributing any cash dividends or bonus shares.
  • CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio — Basel III):
  • Must be maintained at ≥ 11.5\%.
  • If CAR drops below 11.5\%, NRB imposes Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) and halts all dividend declarations.

11. Statutory Regulations, Taxes, Fees & Settlement Operations

11.1 T+2 Rolling Settlement Cycle

  • Mechanics:
  • Day T: Trade executed.
  • Day T+1: Clearing & broker transfer.
  • Day T+2: Shares credited to Demat; funds settled.
  • Strict Constraint: You cannot sell your shares on Day T or Day T+1. If a stock drops after you buy, you are legally locked into holding the position for 2 business days.

  • Loan Ceiling: Bank share loans are capped at 70\% of the 180-day average price or current LTP (whichever is lower).
