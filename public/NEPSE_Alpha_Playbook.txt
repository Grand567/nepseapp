TIER 1: NON-NEGOTIABLE CAPITAL PROTECTORS (Highest Reliability: 90%+ Weight)
These features protect you from severe portfolio drawdowns.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. CASH DEFENSE MODE (Market Breadth Filter)
   - Where to find: Top Status Bar & Predictor Hub
   - How it works: Evaluates real-time Market Breadth (Advance/Decline ratio,
     New Highs vs New Lows, volume distribution across all sub-indices).
   - The Statistical Edge: During bear market regimes (market breadth < 40%),
     over 75% of technical chart breakouts FAIL and reverse into losses.
   - NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE: When Cash Defense Mode is ACTIVE, DO NOT BUY ANY
     BREAKOUT. Keep 70% to 80% of your total portfolio in pure cash.

2. CHASE CAP GUARD (+2.5% Maximum Limit)
   - Where to find: Entry/Exit Analyzer & Stock Details Card
   - How it works: Calculates the exact percentage distance between the ideal
     technical pivot entry and the current live market price.
   - The Statistical Edge: Because of the T+2 settlement freeze, buying a stock
     already up +4% to +8% on Day 1 makes you easy exit liquidity for swing
     traders who bought earlier.
   - NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE: If current price > Entry Pivot + 2.5%, THE TRADE IS
     CANCELLED. Never chase gap-ups into resistance.

3. PROMOTER SHARE LOCK-IN EXPIRY RADAR (60-Day Blackout)
   - Where to find: Stock Information, Fundamentals & Safety Audit
   - How it works: Tracks the regulatory 3-year promoter share lock-in expiry
     window following company IPOs (especially Hydropower and Microfinance).
   - The Statistical Edge: When promoter shares unlock, floating supply often
     doubles or triples overnight as initial promoters offload shares, causing
     protracted price drops of 25% to 50%.
   - NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE: Automatic blacklist. Never enter a swing trade on any
     stock within 60 days of its promoter lock-in expiry date.

4. BROKER LBAS (Large-Block Absorption Share & Floorsheet Flow)
   - Where to find: Floorsheet Analytics & Stock Information Tab
   - How it works: Analyzes daily transactions to distinguish retail lot orders
     from high-volume block orders placed by leading institutional brokers
     (e.g., Broker 58, 45, 34, 17, 49).
   - The Statistical Edge: Retail chart patterns without institutional backing
     frequently fail. Price increases supported by sustained institutional
     accumulation have high multi-day follow-through.
   - NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE: Only enter high-conviction trades when top 3 buyer
     brokers control >= 35% of daily turnover and maintain positive net buy flow.

TIER 3: INFORMATIONAL / DANGEROUS TRAPS (Do NOT Trade in Isolation)
Features that retail traders misuse, leading to avoidable losses.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. STANDALONE OSCILLATORS (RSI, MACD, Stochastics)
   - Trap: In speculative NEPSE runs, an RSI > 70 can stay "overbought" for weeks
     while a stock doubles in price. Conversely, an RSI < 30 ("oversold") can drop
     another 30%. Never buy or sell based solely on an oscillator without volume
     and floorsheet confirmation.

2. THEORETICAL MONTE CARLO PRICE PROJECTIONS
   - Trap: Statistical random-walk projections assume standard Gaussian curves.
     They cannot foresee sudden regulatory shifts by Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB)
     (e.g., changes to margin loan ceiling caps, CD ratios, or interest rates).

3. UNADJUSTED HISTORICAL CHARTS
   - Trap: Failure to adjust historical charts for bonus share splits and right
     shares will render support and resistance levels completely invalid.
