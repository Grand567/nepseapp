📚 NEPSE Financial Academy & Trading Rules

NEPSE Trading Hours & Sessions

• Pre-Open: 10:30 AM – 10:45 AM (Orders matched at single opening price, ±5% price band).

• Continuous Trading: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Regular session, max ±15% daily price circuit limit under updated SEBON rules; historically ±10%).

• Market Circuit Breakers: Level 1 (5% Index move in first 2 hrs = 15-min halt); Level 2 (8% Index move = trading halt for remainder of the day).

SEBON Commission Structure

• <= NPR 50,000: 0.40%

• NPR 50,001 – 500,000: 0.37%

• NPR 500,001 – 2,000,000: 0.34%

• NPR 2,000,001 – 10,000,000: 0.30%

• > NPR 10,000,000: 0.27%

• SEBON Fee: 0.015% | DP Fee: NPR 25

Technical & Fundamental Analysis

• RSI (14): Momentum oscillator (0-100). <30 indicates oversold, >70 overbought.

• MACD: Moving Average Convergence Divergence trend and signal crossover indicator.

• WACC: Weighted Average Cost of Capital required by MeroShare for calculating Capital Gains Tax.

📖 Complete NEPSE Beginner's Guide

Step-by-step masterclass on investing in Nepal Share Market.

1. Opening Accounts (Demat & MeroShare)

Visit any bank or licensed DP to open a Demat account and activate MeroShare portal (NPR 50/year). Your 16-digit BOID is your unique electronic share locker.

2. TMS (Trade Management System) Registration

Register with any of the 62+ registered SEBON brokers (e.g. Broker #58 Naasa, #45 Imperial, #34 Vision). Provide KYC, link your bank account, and receive login credentials to place orders.

3. Trading Sessions & Price Bands

• Pre-Open (10:30 - 10:45 AM): Single opening price matching (±5% price band).
• Continuous (11:00 AM - 3:00 PM): Regular trading session with max ±15% daily price circuit limit (Updated SEBON Rule; historically ±10%).
• Market Circuit: 5% move triggers 15m halt; 8% move halts trading for the day.

4. Order Settlement (T+2) & WACC

Shares are credited/debited on T+2 working days. When selling, calculate WACC on MeroShare to pay exact Capital Gains Tax (7.5% short-term, 5% long-term) and EDIS transfer shares.
