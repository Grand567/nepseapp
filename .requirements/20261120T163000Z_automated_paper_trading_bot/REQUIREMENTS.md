## 2. To Be

1. **Autonomous Execution Bot Engine:**
   - Evaluates active algorithmic signals (Minervini Stage 2 breakouts, Smart-Money ignition, Operator accumulation) and automatically executes sized paper positions if the bot toggle is enabled and risk parameters are satisfied.
2. **Multi-Stage Trailing Stop-Loss Ratchet Engine:**
   - **Stage 0 (Initial Risk):** Stop-loss set at entry risk floor (e.g., -4% to -5% below entry or swing low).
   - **Stage 1 (Breakeven Armor):** As soon as price reaches +1.0R (or +5%), automatically ratchet the stop-loss up to Breakeven (+0.6% buffer to cover SEBON brokerage, DP fee, and SEBON levy).
   - **Stage 2 (Target 1 Partial Profit & Ratchet):** When price reaches Target 1 (+1.5R to +2.0R), execute 50% partial profit take, and immediately ratchet the stop-loss on the remaining 50% shares to the +1.0R gain floor.
   - **Stage 3 (Trailing Runner):** For the remaining shares, dynamically trail the stop-loss behind the 5-day EMA or rising swing low until Target 2 (+2.5R to +3.0R) is hit.
3. **NEPSE Compliance & Invariant Guarantees:**
   - Strictly enforces mandatory NEPSE T+2 Demat Settlement lock before permitting any sell execution (`holdSessions >= 2`).
   - Deducts all statutory friction: 5-tier SEBON broker commission, 0.015% SEBON fee, Rs. 25 DP fee, 7.5% Short-Term CGT, and slippage.
   - Enforces ±15% circuit breaker freeze protection: orders are blocked if a stock is frozen at upper circuit (no sellers) or lower circuit (no buyers).
4. **UI Bot Workstation in `PaperTradingJournalScreen.tsx`:**
   - "🤖 Auto-Bot Mode: ACTIVE / PAUSED" toggle with status badge.
   - Configurable risk parameters: Max Position Size (% of capital), Risk Per Trade (1%, 2%, 3%), and Trailing Ratchet Policy.
   - Visual Ratchet Badges on open positions: `🛡️ INITIAL RISK`, `⚓ BREAKEVEN LOCKED`, `💰 PROFIT LOCKED (+1R)`, `🎯 RUNNER ACTIVE`.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-1:** Position moving from Rs. 500 to Rs. 530 (+6%, > +1R) must ratchet stop-loss to at least Rs. 503 (breakeven + statutory friction buffer).
- **AC-2:** Position touching Target 1 must bank 50% shares and move stop-loss to >= entry price + 0.5R.
- **AC-3:** Demat settlement lock must prevent selling before `holdSessions >= 2` regardless of price action.
- **AC-4:** Upper circuit stocks (+14.95%) must be rejected from new BUY execution with clear reason logging.
- **AC-5:** UI toggle in `PaperTradingJournalScreen.tsx` must persist state across refreshes and render zero `NaN`, `undefined`, or frozen skeletons.
